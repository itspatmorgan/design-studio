## When to use

Use tabs to switch related content in one place. Compose `Tabs`, `TabsList`, `TabsTrigger`, and `TabsContent`; match trigger and panel `value` props. Give the list an accessible label.

Use `defaultValue` for initial selection or `value` and `onValueChange` for controlled selection. Choose `variant="line"` on the list for underline tabs. Disabled tabs are skipped. Arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space activates the focused tab.

See [source and adaptations](/systems/v4576ka1mbpbqdp1/context/library).
