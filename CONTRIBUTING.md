# Contributing

Design Studio is in early beta. You get the source code, and you're encouraged to customize your own studio to fit your needs.

Share what you build, ask questions, and offer feedback in the [GitHub community](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio/discussions).

If you find a bug, [report it](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) with the steps to reproduce it and the version you're on.

## Propose an improvement

Open a [feature proposal](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio/issues/new?template=feature-proposal.yml) before or after you build it. Describe the problem, your solution, and how to check it. If you built it, link your branch and show before and after; a short video helps for interactions. Keep a proposal to one focused change, follow the repository's `AGENTS.md`, and run `pnpm check` and `pnpm test`.

## What happens next

A maintainer triages each issue: whether it fits Design Studio's principles, where it belongs, and when to do it. You'll hear the outcome on your issue: fixing it now, keeping it open for later, or closing it with the reason. If it goes ahead, the maintainer may adapt the implementation to match the rest of the platform rather than asking you for another round. Your commits keep your authorship, and the changelog and release notes credit you.

When the change ships, you'll get a reply with what changed from your proposal and why. Replies drafted by an AI assistant for a maintainer say so.
